Maybank and its insurance arm Etiqa Life Insurance Berhad completed a pilot trade in Malaysian Government Securities (MGS) using tokenised deposits.
The bank described it as Malaysia’s first trade in tokenised MGS.
It ran the pilot under Bank Negara Malaysia’s Digital Asset Innovation Hub.
The two companies tested a programmable delivery-versus-payment process across blockchain and conventional systems.
The process linked payment to the delivery of the securities so neither could go through on its own.
Maybank held the underlying cash and securities in custody.
Dato’ Sri Khairussaleh Ramli, President and Group CEO of Maybank said,
“We see significant long-term opportunities in strengthening cross-border flows as ASEAN progresses towards deeper digital integration through initiatives such as the ASEAN Digital Economy Framework Agreement (DEFA).
Our next phase will focus on scaling up our infrastructure to advance commercial applications, including tokenised bonds, deposits and other digital assets.”
Maybank also helped arrange Malaysia’s first tokenised sukuk issuance by sovereign wealth fund Khazanah Nasional Berhad earlier this year.
