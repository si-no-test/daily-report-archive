Anthropic Unveils Office Suite to Challenge Microsoft Ahead of Landmark IPO
Anthropic is releasing a suite of workplace applications designed to integrate artificial intelligence directly into daily business operations, escalating competition with major backers Microsoft and Alphabet’s Google as the startup prepares for an initial public offering later (IPO) this month, according to a report by Bloomberg .
The product, unveiled at a company event Tuesday (Nov. 10), introduces tools including a video call assistant, a data dashboard service, and Claude Sheets — a spreadsheet application resembling Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets with Anthropic’s AI embedded throughout. Bloomberg reported that several of the new tools are accessible in beta format for paid subscribers.
The expansion marks a strategic push by the Claude developer to broaden adoption among enterprise business professionals beyond software engineers, where it previously built strong demand for coding and debugging tasks.
However, Nicholas Lin, an Anthropic product leader, characterized Claude Sheets as a “fairly basic” offering not intended for advanced financial modeling, noting that third-party plug-ins into established spreadsheet software remain more effective for complex work.
Anthropic also announced functionality allowing corporate software vendors including Adobe, Atlassian, and HubSpot to embed versions of their existing applications within Claude. Shares of Microsoft fell 3% in New York trading by early Tuesday afternoon following the announcement.
The product launch follows surging top-line financial growth for the company. According to a regulatory filing cited by Bloomberg, Anthropic achieved an annualized revenue run rate of $81 billion as of Oct. 31, a $10 billion increase from late September. The AI developer is seeking a fully diluted valuation of about $1.4 trillion in its upcoming IPO.
The enterprise office push and $1.4 trillion IPO valuation target arrive against a backdrop of accelerating model competition and tech market volatility, according to reporting by PYMNTS. Just ahead of the office suite rollout, Anthropic introduced its technical Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 models to coordinate complex multi-agent corporate workflows, competing directly with Meta’s newly unveiled Muse Ember agent model.
Concurrently, a $2.5 trillion Nasdaq sell-off sparked investor skepticism over Anthropic’s target pricing, with key investors indicating the valuation could settle closer to $1 trillion or face listing delays if broader market turbulence continues.
