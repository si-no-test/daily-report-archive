Ameris Bancorp to buy Florida bank for $180M
Acquiring Bank of Central Florida would give Ameris the No. 5 deposit position among community and regional banks in the Sunshine State, the lender said.
Ameris Bancorp will buy Lakeland, Florida-based Bank of Central Florida Holding Company in a $180 million all-stock deal, the banks said Monday .
The holding company of $1.2 billion-asset Bank of Central Florida will push Atlanta-based Ameris Bank’s asset total to roughly $29.7 billion.
The deal would give Ameris added scale in “strategically important and demographically attractive” central Florida markets, according to an investor presentation . It’s projected to close in the first quarter of 2027.
In addition to $1.2 billion in assets, Bank of Central Florida counts $851 million in loans and $1.1 billion in deposits. The deal would bring Ameris to the No. 5 spot by deposit share for community and regional banks in fast-growing Florida.
Ameris entered Florida in 2001 and roughly 50 of its 163 branches are in the Sunshine State. The lender found acquisition targets in multiple Florida banks in recent years, including Atlantic Coast Financial Corp. in 2018 and Jacksonville Bancorp in 2015.
“This proposed transaction will enhance our position in Central Florida by adding a meaningful presence along the fast-growing I-4 corridor between Tampa and Orlando, while also strengthening our banking operations in the Tampa market itself,” Ameris CEO Palmer Proctor said in a prepared statement.
“The combination brings together Bank of Central Florida’s long-standing customer relationships and local focus with Ameris’s broader product suite and larger balance sheet, creating a platform for continued growth while maintaining a commitment to serving customers throughout the region,” he said.
Paul Noris, Bank of Central Florida’s founder, CEO and chair, will assume leadership of Ameris’ Lakeland and Tampa markets – including existing Ameris branches – when the deal closes.
“I’m proud of what our team has built and am grateful for the trust our clients and communities have placed in us. In Ameris, we’ve found a partner that shares our commitment to relationship banking,” Noris said in a prepared statement.
Under the terms of the deal, Bank of Central Florida shareholders will receive 0.6279 shares of Ameris common stock for each outstanding share they own, for an implied value of $51.80 per share based on Ameris’ closing price of $82.50 on Thursday.
Bank of Central Florida is a cultural and geographic fit for Ameris, according to the investor presentation, and the target has a strong core deposit franchise, improving Ameris’ loan-to-deposit ratio with excess deposits of $272 million.
Additionally, the Florida bank has low levels of historical net charge-offs, and the deal comes with “manageable” tangible book value per share dilution, with an earnback period under three years, Ameris said in the presentation.
Truist Securities analysts led by John McDonald called the deal “a targeted tuck-in.”
The transaction is “an opportunistic deployment of Ameris' excess capital, consistent with its intention to drive profitable organic growth over time, as the deal should be additive to both its overall Florida franchise and funding profile after close,” according to a Monday note sent to investors.
Notably, M&A wasn’t high on Ameris’ capital deployment list, at least last month, Proctor indicated on a third-quarter earnings call Oct. 23.
“Most of the targets that we like are not for sale. And so it takes something very – it's got to be a cultural fit. It's got to be a financial fit,” Proctor said. “And unless something like that unique unicorn kind of came along, that would probably be last on our list [of how to deploy capital].”
