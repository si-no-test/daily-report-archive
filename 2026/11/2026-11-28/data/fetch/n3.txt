Neumetria , which provides a behavioral understanding layer for financial platforms and AI agents, has entered a partnership with Salt Edge , an open banking solutions provider.
The collaboration targets clients across Europe and North America, combining consented account data with interpretive analysis to give banks, lenders , fintechs , and AI agent developers a clearer, more current picture of individual customers before they extend credit, engage, or take automated actions. Institutions retain full control over their own policies and decisions.
Open banking has simplified access to account information, yet raw data alone rarely equates to genuine insight.
Temporary surges in funds can be hard to separate from sustained changes in financial circumstances.
Likewise, it can be difficult to tell whether a customer is establishing a new pattern of behaviour or departing from a long-standing one. Income streams, spending habits, savings balances, and credit obligations are typically scattered across multiple accounts and different institutions.
Assembling a consistent, up-to-date view of this activity demands substantial connectivity infrastructure that is expensive and complex for most organisations to build and maintain internally.
Salt Edge addresses the connectivity challenge by supplying consented and normalised account data drawn from a customer’s various banks through a single integration.
Its data aggregation service connects to more than 5,000 financial institutions in over 50 countries, including PSD2 coverage of more than 2,700 European institutions.
Rather than managing bank-by-bank connections, Neumetria can build on this consistent feed of current account activity.
This frees Neumetria to concentrate on the subsequent layer: transforming transaction streams into an evidence-based portrait of the person behind the accounts.
That portrait encompasses income patterns, liquidity position, existing commitments, and overall financial capacity.
Customers also benefit from a simpler experience.
Instead of uploading paper or PDF statements, they can link their accounts in a few clicks.
Together, the two companies enable clients to ground AI agents in a multi-bank perspective of income, obligations, and liquidity before any action is taken.
Proposed actions can be assessed against an institution’s own policy, with a retained record of the underlying data and rules that produced each outcome.
Current capacity and liquidity measures can sit alongside traditional bureau data , helping lenders support thin-file customers, gig workers, and those with irregular income.
Credit and repayment schedules can be aligned more closely with observed income cycles.
Early indicators of financial strain can be identified before they escalate into arrears, and systems can determine when it is appropriate to prompt a customer versus when it is better to remain silent.
Amr Mohamed , founder and chief executive officer of Neumetria, noted that AI agents require more than a simple balance figure before they act. Salt Edge supplies consented access to activity across a customer ’s accounts, while Neumetria converts that activity into a current assessment of income, commitments, and liquidity.
The institution continues to set the policy and remains accountable for outcomes. Neumetria makes those rules executable and ensures the resulting decisions can be reconstructed later.
Alina Beleuta , chief growth officer at Salt Edge, observed that AI is only as powerful and credible as the data it can access responsibly.
Open banking places users in control and allows consent-based, real-time financial information to complement conventional credit histories.
In combination, the approach can help lenders evaluate risk more accurately, broaden access to financing , and give individuals a portable financial profile that travels with them across countries and continents.Europe and North America represent the two priority regions for the partnership.
In Europe, Neumetria is moving into additional markets that feature distinct regulatory environments, especially as the region transitions from PSD2 toward PSD3 and the Payment Services Regulation.
Relying on Salt Edge’s single API avoids months of work building and maintaining separate bank integrations in each jurisdiction.
The companies also intend to collaborate beyond Europe , including in North America, where Salt Edge has recently expanded its data aggregation coverage.
By pairing broad, consented data connectivity with a specialised behavioural understanding layer, the partnership offers financial institutions and AI agent builders a practical route to more informed risk assessment, better product recommendations, and more precise automated actions—while preserving institutional oversight and decision authority at every step.
