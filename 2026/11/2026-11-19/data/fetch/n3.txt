Banking digitalization expert Veritran has acquired personalized marketing automation fintech PrismaCampaigns . Financial terms of the deal are undisclosed.
Florida-based PrismaCampaigns was founded in 2017 to help banks and credit unions leverage customer data to create and deliver tailored communications to create targeted customer experiences at scale and ultimately strengthen customer relationships. The company, which has maintained double-digit annual growth, serves more than 85 financial institutions and supports more than 6.2 million customer relationships. Of its bank clients, more than 60 are located in the US, which is a strategic market for Argentina-based Veritran.
“PrismaCampaigns opens doors in markets where we want to expand and adds a solution that further strengthens our value proposition,” said Veritran CEO and Co-founder Marcelo González. “We are excited to begin this journey together.”
Veritran anticipates that PrismaCampaigns will expand its portfolio of solutions, add specialized expertise in marketing technology for the financial services industry, and will add clients in the US, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Honduras, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Trinidad and Tobago, and Uruguay.
Following the acquisition, PrismaCampaigns will maintain its leadership and continue to serve its customers and channel partners. The deal will allow Veritran to combine its digital banking technology with PrismaCampaigns’ customer segmentation and marketing automation capabilities. PrismaCampaigns enables financial institutions to deliver personalized communications and offers across mobile, web, email, SMS, and push notifications. By adding these capabilities to its portfolio, Veritran can help banks and credit unions move beyond providing digital banking services to delivering more targeted, relevant customer experiences.
“Over the years, we built PrismaCampaigns alongside our clients and partners with a clear focus: helping banks and credit unions better understand their customers and members and communicate with them in relevant ways,” said PrismaCampaigns Co-Founder and CEO Guillermo Winkler. “Joining Veritran gives us the scale and support needed to accelerate our roadmap and reach new markets, while preserving what makes us different: close customer relationships and an open platform that adapts to each institution’s needs.”
Founded in 2005 and headquartered in Buenos Aires, Argentina, Veritran helps financial institutions develop and deploy digital banking solutions through its low-code technology platform. The company enables banks and other financial services providers to build digital experiences while reducing development time and complexity. Veritran most recently demonstrated its technology at FinovateFall 2021 , where it showcased how financial institutions can use its low-code platform to build and launch a white-label digital wallet, accelerating time to market while delivering a streamlined customer experience.
